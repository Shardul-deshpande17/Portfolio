About me

I'm a cybersecurity student building a career in Security Operations, with a pull toward the blue team side: monitoring, alert triage, incident investigation and threat detection.

Most of my hands-on work is digging into security events, tracing suspicious activity, spotting indicators of compromise and writing detection logic in Splunk, alongside analysing network traffic in Wireshark to see what's really happening on the wire. I completed the TryHackMe SOC Level 1 path, which gave me practical experience in SIEM investigation, packet analysis and log correlation, and I keep sharpening it through further labs and self-directed investigations.

I'm looking for a SOC Analyst (L1) role where I can put this foundation to work in a live security operations environment and keep growing as a defender.

"It's Downtime!" Blue Team CTF

TryHackMe · My first dedicated defensive CTF

Team rank 335 · played solo

What it involved

A set of warm-up exercises followed by five CTF challenges where I had to investigate evidence and decide what to examine next. The warm-up built confidence; the five challenges showed me how much I still have to learn.

Areas covered

  • Disk images
  • Extracting embedded files
  • Encrypted artifacts & hashes
  • Command-line tools
  • Digital forensics

What I learned

  • Following an investigation is different from running the whole process confidently on my own.
  • I used extra learning resources when stuck, and treated those moments as study time.
  • Finding my gaps was the real result, and I now know what to practise.
  • Next step: redo the hard parts independently and write my own investigation playbook.
